        How the hours are counted

        One home time zone

        Each fanbase has one representative time zone, usually its capital or largest city. Every kick-off is converted to that time.

        Two hours per match

        A match counts as 120 minutes from kick-off, which covers both halves, half-time and stoppage time. It is counted once it has finished.

        The night window

        Any of those minutes between 23:00 and 07:00 local time count as lost sleep for every supporter of the team.

        Fanbase size

        Totals multiply the hours for each supporter by the national population, as a rough guide to how many supporters there are. Nobody's viewing was measured.
